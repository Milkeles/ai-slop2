1. **Part II — “He gave her the rucksack anyway.”**  
   **Problem:** The false Ines has no weight or substance. Tavi would notice when the rucksack passed through her hands; Pell’s actual collection of it is missing.  
   **Specific fix:** Position Pell on the garage roof, with the illusion covering his body and hands. Let him physically take the rucksack while the false Ines remains silent.

2. **Part III — “He let go, and felt his balance drain out of him”**  
   **Problem:** Nobody has discovered or explained how to return folds. The children execute the exact procedure at the climax without learning it, weakening the resolution.  
   **Specific fix:** During Dobri’s first visit, have him accidentally return one fold by following its thread and releasing it. Give him a small memory loss and Gran a recovered word, establishing both the method and its price.

3. **Part III — “He burned them.”**  
   **Problem:** Burning snapped folds is allowed, but its mechanism is unexplained. Ordinary power use consumes energy without removing folds, so producing light alone cannot explain Pell’s permanent sacrifice.  
   **Specific fix:** Foreshadow during Pell’s confession that snapped folds can be deliberately destroyed. At the climax, distinguish that irreversible choice from ordinary light use, so readers understand why his power and memories disappear.

4. **Part III — “He’ll be on the gantry,” she said. “Where it came up.”**  
   **Problem:** They found the Coil on a lower quarry ledge, not the gantry. Pell’s overheard clue therefore points somewhere else, making Ines’s crucial deduction unsupported.  
   **Specific fix:** Establish in Part I that the discovery ledge lies directly beneath the gantry, then make Pell’s clue identify the structure above it. Alternatively, change his mutter to name the gantry directly.

5. **Part III — “Ines looked ahead and saw nothing.”**  
   **Problem:** Ines is still sitting on the gantry when her foresight fails. The bible limits that failure to being inside the Fold; entering it should not erase her ability beforehand.  
   **Specific fix:** Move the failed attempt until after she closes her eyes and enters. Let her choose to enter knowing Pell’s warning, then experience the blankness herself.

The donor smells make the twist feel earned, and Pip’s rescue gives keeping the Coil a convincing emotional justification.  
The characters have distinct voices: Ines counts, Tavi jokes and bargains, and Dobri listens until speaking matters.  
Gran’s final recording turns the memory cost into a tender, concrete ending that also circles back to the opening.
