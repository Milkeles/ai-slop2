# Story Bible — The Folding

## 1. Three alternative directions

- **A. Static.** An old valve radio in a repair shop picks up a station only four friends can hear; listening folds their brains. Tone: eerie, funny. Twist: the "station" is the town's sleeping minds, and the friends are draining them.
- **B. Bloom.** Pale jellyfish appear in a flooded quarry; their stings fold the brain. Tone: summer adventure turning creepy. Twist: the bloom is one brainless creature that thinks *through* the kids' new folds.
- **C. Low Water (chosen).** A drought exposes a glowing ammonite fossil; touching it folds the friends' brains. Tone: warm, funny, then frightening. Twist: the fossil makes nothing. It *moves* folds, and each one comes from the person who loves you most.

**Why C:** the stakes sit inside the friends' own families. The twist is fair because it is set up with clues the audience can catch aloud (smells, matching symptoms). The cost is written into the rules and paid in full at the end. The antagonist is a mirror, not a monster. All three key objects exist as free 3D models.

## 2. Title and logline

**The Folding** (the brain folds, and in the end the characters "fold": they give in and give back).

When a drought exposes a glowing fossil at the bottom of a quarry, three friends start growing new folds in their brains, and powers to match, until they learn that every fold has an owner and someone they love is paying for it.

## 3. Magic rules (final)

1. **Source:** the Coil, an ammonite fossil the size of a dinner plate, from the bottom of Cutter's Bowl quarry. Its suture lines glow faintly. It only surfaces when drought drops the water (last time: 1979). It does not create folds. It moves them.
2. **Bond:** the first people to touch it after it leaves the water "wake" it, and it answers only to them. To anyone else it is just a stone.
3. **Folding:** each time a holder presses a palm to the spiral, one fold forms in them. It feels like pressure behind the eyes, a click inside the skull like a knuckle cracking, a flash of pale ridges, and a smell they half-recognise.
4. **Hidden rule (the twist):** every new fold is taken from the person who loves the holder most, and from the same brain region. Donors feel nothing. They just get worse at what that region does. The smell belongs to the donor.
5. **Region → power** (only borrowed folds give power): **Frontal (Ines):** sees ahead, from 2 up to about 10 seconds. **Temporal (Dobri):** hears echoes of words once spoken in a place (older echoes cost more) and, near a person, hears memories in motion. **Cerebellum (Tavi):** perfect balance, speed and reflexes. **Occipital (Pell):** makes and bends light, so glows, images and illusions that look solid but have no weight and no sound.
6. **Energy:** folds make energy, and using a power burns it as heat (hot ears, a metal taste). Rest, sleep and cold water refill it. Using a power never adds or takes folds. Only touching the Coil does.
7. **Overuse:** first a nosebleed, then lost recent memories (minutes to hours), and beyond that the user's own native folds begin to smooth.
8. **Limits:** Ines sees only her own surroundings, and she sees illusions too, though she also sees what happens when someone trusts them. Dobri hears words and memories, never thoughts. Pell's illusions are light only.
9. **Threads:** each borrowed fold stays tied to its donor by a thread, visible only in the Fold. Threads thin with time, and old donors' threads thin fastest. When a thread snaps, the fold belongs to the borrower forever and the donor's loss is permanent. A fold with a snapped thread can never go home. It can only be burned away.
10. **The Fold:** anyone carrying borrowed folds who touches the Coil with eyes closed enters the Fold, an inner landscape of ridges and valleys. Borrowed folds glow amber. Every thread passes through a great stone spiral at the centre. Time does not run forward there, so foresight shows nothing.
11. **Return:** walk a thread to its end and let go. The fold goes home, and every memory the borrower made in it goes home with it. Walking burns energy.
12. **Breaking:** if the Coil breaks, every thread snaps at once and all loans become permanent.

## 4. Characters

### Ines Marek (14) — the planner
- **Personality:** organised, quick, bossy when scared, dry sense of humour.
- **Motivation:** keep everything under control. Her mum, Hana, is a single parent and an ER nurse on night shifts, so Ines runs the house.
- **Power:** frontal lobe, foresight. **Donor:** her mum, who starts losing her planning and judgement.
- **Key object:** a cheap black digital wristwatch she uses to time how far ahead she sees.
- **Inner conflict:** she needs to know what comes next, but nothing she loves can be planned.
- **Arc:** in I she plans the climb and loves knowing. In II she makes rules and loses whole afternoons. In III she guides Tavi second by second, then has to walk into the Fold blind, where foresight doesn't work.
- **Visual line:** A 14-year-old girl, slim, medium height, olive skin, dark brown hair in a blunt chin-length bob with a straight fringe, thick dark eyebrows, brown eyes. She wears a mustard-yellow zip-up windbreaker over a grey T-shirt, black cargo trousers, white trainers, and a black plastic digital wristwatch on her left wrist. A short list is written in blue ballpoint on the back of her hand.

### Dobri Anev (13) — the listener
- **Personality:** quiet, observant, funny in a whisper, avoids conflict.
- **Motivation:** his parents work abroad and his Gran, Nevena, raised him. She used to be "the town's memory" and recorded old people's stories on tape. Now she forgets things, and he wants her back.
- **Power:** temporal lobe, echoes. **Donor:** Gran, who loses words, names and memories.
- **Key object:** Gran's silver portable cassette recorder, with old over-ear headphones.
- **Inner conflict:** he believes he is only a listener, never the one who speaks. He touches the Coil most because deeper echoes let him hear Gran's old stories, so his guilt is the biggest.
- **Arc:** in I he records and notices. In II he discovers the twist at Gran's bedside. In III he speaks the words that stop Pell, returns Gran's folds and loses the summer.
- **Visual line:** A 13-year-old boy, short and thin, pale skin with freckles across the nose, messy black curly hair, large round tortoiseshell glasses, grey-green eyes. He wears an oversized faded green knitted cardigan over a white T-shirt, brown corduroy trousers and scuffed brown boots. Old over-ear headphones hang around his neck, their cable running to a silver portable cassette recorder clipped to his belt.

### Tavi Doran (14) — the runner
- **Personality:** loud, generous, impatient, makes jokes about his own bad luck.
- **Motivation:** he was the fastest runner in school until he fell from the quarry gantry in spring and tore his knee. He wants to be fast again before the athletics trials.
- **Power:** cerebellum, balance and speed. **Donor:** his sister Pip (8), who adores him and starts losing her balance and her swimming.
- **Key object:** a black hinged knee brace.
- **Inner conflict:** being special again, or being a good brother.
- **Arc:** in I the brace comes off and he saves Pip. In II he wants "three more days", and his choice lets Pell steal the Coil. In III he crosses the gantry he fell from, returns Pip's folds first and buckles the brace back on.
- **Visual line:** A 14-year-old boy, tall and long-limbed, dark brown skin, close-cropped black hair with a thin shaved line above the left ear, brown eyes, a small plaster on his chin. He wears a red sleeveless running vest, black athletic shorts, a black hinged knee brace on his right knee, mismatched socks (one white, one striped) and worn blue running shoes.

### Augustin "Gus" Pell (60) — antagonist, keeper of the Weighhouse
- **Personality:** gruff, precise, lonely, ashamed.
- **Motivation:** he found the Coil in the 1979 drought, aged 13. His donor was his mother, who stopped recognising faces. He waited too long, her threads snapped, and she died not knowing him. He threw the Coil into deep water. Now he wants to break it so no other family suffers. He believes return is impossible.
- **Power:** occipital lobe, light and illusion. It is still active, because the folds are his mother's and can never go home.
- **Key object:** an old slide projector, where at night he makes his mother's face out of light.
- **Inner conflict:** he says he is protecting the children, but breaking the Coil would also let him keep the last of her.
- **Arc:** in I he warns them ("It doesn't give. Nothing gives."). In II he confesses, then steals the Coil using an illusion of Ines. In III he burns the last of his mother's folds to light the Fold for the children.
- **Visual line:** A tall, stooped man of about sixty, gaunt face, pale skin, deep lines around the eyes, thin white hair combed back, short white beard. He wears a long brown canvas work coat with many pockets over a buttoned grey wool waistcoat and collarless white shirt, dark trousers and heavy boots. A brass jeweller's loupe hangs on a cord around his neck, and his fingertips are white with chalk dust.

*Minor characters, no portraits:* Gran Nevena Aneva (about 80: small, short white hair, lilac cardigan, smells of quince jam), Hana Marek (smells of hospital hand gel), Pip Doran (smells of strawberry bubblegum).

## 5. Locations

### Cutter's Bowl
- **What:** an abandoned limestone quarry above the town of Limeburn, half-flooded. A rusted conveyor gantry reaches out over the water.
- **Why it matters:** the Coil is found here, Tavi once fell from the gantry, and the climax happens here.
- **Parts:** I, II, III.
- **Visual line:** A deep abandoned limestone quarry with stepped white and cream cliff terraces. The turquoise water at the bottom is low from drought, leaving a pale ring of bare rock and cracked mud ledges. A rusted orange conveyor gantry on steel legs reaches out over the water from the rim. Dry yellow grass and thistles line the top edge, beside a broken chain-link fence, under a hot late-August sky.

### The Weighhouse
- **What:** the old weighbridge office at the quarry gate, now a one-room fossil museum where Pell lives.
- **Why it matters:** the first warning, the confession and the 1979 echoes all happen here, and the Coil ends here under glass.
- **Parts:** I, II, epilogue.
- **Visual line:** A small single-storey red-brick building beside a disused weighbridge platform at a quarry gate, with a corrugated iron roof and one tall arched window. Inside is a single room crowded with glass display cases of fossils and rocks, handwritten paper labels and wooden specimen drawers. A slide projector on a metal trolley throws a square of white light onto the wall, with dust floating in the beam.

### The Fold
- **What:** the inner landscape that holders enter by touching the Coil with their eyes closed.
- **Why it matters:** the twist is seen here (the threads) and the folds are returned here.
- **Parts:** I (a flash), II, III.
- **Visual line:** An endless dreamlike landscape seen from inside a brain: rounded pale pink-white ridges rising and falling like folded dunes, with deep narrow valleys between them glowing soft blue. Thin amber threads of light run from some ridges across the land toward a dark horizon. In the centre a huge grey stone spiral sits half-sunk in the ground, under a dim violet sky without sun or stars.

## 6. Plot outline

### Part I — Low Water (about 1,200 words)
1. **Cutter's Bowl, late August (all three).** The water is the lowest since 1979 ("Gran says"). Tavi, banned from climbing, spots a spiral on a newly exposed ledge. Ines plans the climb down. They pry out the Coil and all three touch it: a click, a flash of ridges, and a smell each, which they laugh off (hand gel, bubblegum, quince). *Change:* the Coil is awake.
2. **Three homes, next morning (cross-cut).** Ines catches a falling mug two seconds before it slips, while Mum, home from a night shift, has for the first time ever forgotten lunch money and left the gas on. Dobri hears yesterday's words in an empty kitchen, and Gran can't find the word "spoon". Tavi trips down the stairs and lands like a cat, and Pip says she "forgot how to whistle". *Change:* the powers are real and the clues are planted.
3. **The Weighhouse (trio, Pell).** Pell studies the Coil through his loupe, goes white and offers to buy it. Then he says: "Put it back in the water. It doesn't give. Nothing gives." The projector beam bends toward his hand. Dobri hears an echo of a boy saying "Mum? Mum, it's me." *Change:* someone knows, and they refuse him.
4. **Cutter's Bowl, evening.** They touch the Coil twice more, with the smells again. Tavi takes off his brace and runs the rim. Ines reaches five seconds of foresight and has her first nosebleed, so they dunk their heads in cold water. Pip, who followed them, slips on a crumbling ledge. Ines sees it coming and Tavi gets there in time.
- **Turning point:** the Coil saved Pip, so they decide to keep it, secretly, at Dobri's. (Irony: Pip is paying for it.)

### Part II — Echoes (about 1,450 words)
1. **Bowl, ten days later.** The powers have grown: Ines sees 10 seconds ahead, Tavi runs along the gantry, and Dobri replays echoes a week old. Ines loses a whole afternoon and Dobri's nose bleeds daily. Ines wants a one-touch-a-day rule, and Tavi touches the Coil twice a day. *Change:* the costs show, and so does the craving.
2. **Homes.** Hana is sent home after nearly giving a patient the wrong dose. Pip, a strong swimmer, sinks at the town pool and has to be pulled out. Gran calls Dobri "the boy". *Change:* the stakes rise, but nobody connects them yet.
3. **TWIST, Dobri's flat at night.** Dobri sits by Gran with the Coil in his lap, hoping to hear her old stories, and drops into the Fold for the first time. He sees amber folds, with a thread running from him straight to Gran. The room smells of quince, and he hears her memories moving toward him. "It isn't giving me anything. It's taking her." *Change:* the truth.
4. **The Weighhouse, morning (trio, Pell).** Pell confesses the 1979 story and makes his mother's face out of projector light: "This is her. It's all I kept." Ines works out that every thread passes through the spiral, so breaking the Coil would cut them all. The kids want to give the folds back. Pell answers: "You can't. I tried." (The truth is he waited.) *Change:* the rules are known, and Pell is the obstacle.
5. **Bowl, then Tavi's window.** In a fight, Ines and Dobri want to return the folds tonight. Tavi wants three more days because the trials are on Saturday, and he takes the Coil home. At night "Ines" appears at his window, finger to her lips, holding up a note that says *let me hide it*. He hands it over. Then a text arrives from the real Ines. Dobri, at the window, hears the echo of Pell's mutter: "Where it came up, it gets broken."
- **Turning point:** Pell has the Coil and will break it at the gantry.

### Part III — Return (about 1,350 words)
1. **Streets to the Bowl, midnight.** Tavi confesses and nobody yells. Ines plans the route by foresight. Dobri's nose bleeds, and he can feel Gran's thread thinning as a high whine. *Change:* the three are united again.
2. **CLIMAX, the gantry.** Pell stands at the far end with the Coil on a beam and a sledgehammer. He fills the air with false lights and lays down a false walkway. From the rim Ines calls Tavi's steps: she sees him fall through the fake plank seven seconds ahead and shouts "Not that one, the rusty one." Tavi crosses the gantry he once fell from. As Pell raises the hammer, Dobri goes deep into the echoes, past years, to 1979 on this same gantry. He hears Pell's mother calling and repeats her aloud through the blood: "Gussie, come home. I don't need your face to know you." Pell stops, and Tavi takes the Coil.
3. **CLIMAX, the Fold (all four).** Ines's foresight is blank, so she has to go in without knowing. The Fold is dark and Gran's thread is thin as a hair. Pell burns the last of his mother's folds, the whole Fold turns gold, and her face fades from his memory. Tavi returns Pip's folds first. Ines lets go without a plan. Dobri runs Gran's thread as it frays and lets go just before it snaps, and his summer goes with it.
4. **The gantry at dawn.** They are ordinary again, with nosebleeds and holes in their memory. Ines doesn't know why she's here. Tavi buckles his brace. Dobri doesn't recognise Pell. Pell sits with an empty hand, with no light but calm. The Coil is grey and cold.
- **End of part:** the cost has been paid.

### Epilogue — Quince (about 300 words)
- The Coil sits in a locked case at the Weighhouse, labelled *Ammonite. Found twice. Do not touch.*
- Hana is back at work and reminds Ines of plans Ines made and forgot. Pip tells Tavi she dreamed she ran along the gantry.
- In Gran's kitchen Gran is sharp again, and she remembers what only Dobri lived. She sets the cassette recorder on the table: "You won't remember it, so I'll tell it. You keep the tape." The red record light comes on. (This pays off rule 11: love carries the memory home.)

### Contradiction risks and fixes
- **Pell steals instead of using the Coil:** it answers only to those who woke it (rule 2).
- **The fake "Ines" never speaks:** illusions are light only (rule 5), so she uses a note.
- **Illusions vs foresight:** Ines sees the illusion too, but she also sees Tavi fall through it (rule 8).
- **Ines is blind in the Fold:** time does not run forward there (rule 10).
- **Pell can enter the Fold:** he still carries borrowed folds (rule 10).
- **Pell's power survives:** his mother's threads snapped (rule 9), so his only option in the Fold is to burn his folds, not return them.
- **The clock:** Gran is old, so her threads thin fastest (rule 9). The stealing doesn't cancel the powers, because the folds are already inside the kids (rule 6).
- **Donor symptoms match the region** (rule 4): Gran loses words, Hana loses judgement, Pip loses balance, Pell's mother lost faces.
- **The Coil resurfaces now:** drought, the same as in 1979. Pell threw it into deep water, so he plans to break it rather than throw it again.
- **Memory loss is partial:** only memories made in borrowed folds go home. That is why the friends still know each other but have lost the magic summer.
